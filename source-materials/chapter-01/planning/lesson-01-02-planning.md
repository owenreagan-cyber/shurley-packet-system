# Chapter 1 Lessons 1–2 Planning Source

## Lesson 1: Discussion and Goal Setting

### Core Skills

- Follow discussion expectations.
- Listen respectfully.
- Wait for an appropriate turn.
- Stay on topic.
- Link a comment to another speaker.
- Distinguish short-term and long-term goals.
- Distinguish academic and personal goals.
- Create action steps for a larger goal.

### Linking Comment Starters

- I agree with ___ because...
- I would like to add to what ___ said...
- I have a different idea because...
- When ___ said ___, it made me think...
- Can you explain what you meant by...?

### Goal-Setting Concepts

A long-term goal takes a longer period of time to achieve.

A short-term goal can be achieved soon and can serve as a step toward a long-term goal.

Academic goals focus on learning and schoolwork.

Personal goals focus on habits, character, relationships, hobbies, and responsibilities.

### Student Applications

- Sort example goals by short-term and long-term.
- Sort example goals by academic and personal.
- Choose one academic long-term goal.
- Write two or three short-term action steps.
- Choose one personal long-term goal.
- Write two or three short-term action steps.

## Lesson 2: Journaling and Letter to Future Me

### Core Skills

- Understand the purpose of a journal.
- Generate possible journal topics.
- Reflect on current interests, feelings, and experiences.
- Set personal and academic goals.
- Write a letter to a future self.

### Journal Definition

A journal is a personal written record of thoughts, feelings, experiences, plans, and imagined stories.

### Journal Topic Ideas

- Family
- Friends
- Pets
- Hobbies
- Favorite activities
- Important events
- Future plans
- Worries
- Goals
- Made-up stories

### Letter to Future Me Structure

Students do not need to use exactly three paragraphs.

Use four flexible parts:

1. Who I Am Right Now
2. My Thoughts About Fourth Grade
3. My Goals and Action Steps
4. A Message to My Future Self

### Suggested Planning Questions

#### Who I Am Right Now

- What are your favorite hobbies?
- What are your favorite foods, games, books, or activities?
- What is important to you right now?

#### My Thoughts About Fourth Grade

- What are you excited about?
- What feels new?
- What makes you nervous?
- How will you handle challenges?

#### My Goals and Action Steps

- What is one academic goal?
- What short-term actions will help you reach it?
- What is one personal goal?
- What short-term actions will help you reach it?

#### A Message to My Future Self

- What do you hope you accomplished?
- What do you want your future self to remember?
- What encouragement would you give yourself?

### Envelope Directions

Write:

- First and last name
- Teacher or class
- "Do Not Open Until the Last Week of School"

The teacher stores the letters and returns them near the end of the school year.
