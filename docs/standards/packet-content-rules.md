# Shurley Packet Content Rules

## Audience

All student-facing text must be appropriate for **fourth grade** reading level. Use clear, direct sentences.

## Curriculum Integrity

- Do not silently invent Shurley rules, vocabulary definitions, lesson facts, rule numbers, or classifications.
- New practice questions may be created only when they accurately exercise a **confirmed skill** from source material.
- Do not reproduce an unknown textbook rule-number system.
- Do not use archived materials alone as authority for vocabulary or definitions.
- Historical archive files may inform gaps but must not override supplied Chapter source materials.

## Vocabulary Standard

When vocabulary appears, include all of:

- Word (blank line if teacher-assigned)
- Definition
- Kid-friendly meaning
- Synonym
- Antonym
- Model sentence (teacher edition only, when applicable)
- Student sentence (blank lines)
- Memory picture or visual association (drawing box)

**Inclusion rule:** Do not pre-fill a vocabulary word or definition unless it is directly confirmed in the supplied Chapter source materials for that lesson. Blank templates from Guided Notes remain blank in the student packet. The teacher assigns or confirms vocabulary.

**Analogy examples:** Source analogy lines (e.g., `encourage : cheer :: easy : simple`) may appear as examples because they exist in Guided Notes. They do not confirm a vocabulary definition list.

## Instructional Rhythm

Each lesson follows this sequence where appropriate:

1. **Learn** — Introduce or review the concept.
2. **Practice Together** — Guided practice with teacher or partner support.
3. **Practice Independently** — Student works alone.
4. **Apply** — Real-world or creative application.
5. **Memory Check** — 1–3 brief retrieval questions without looking back.
6. **Teacher Check** — Completion tracking (Complete □, Date, Initials).

Use these exact labels unless a lesson genuinely does not require one.

## Standard Page Components

### Remember / Knowledge Box

Important information appears in a restrained box labeled **Remember**. Keep text brief.

### Memory Check (every lesson)

```
MEMORY CHECK
Without looking back, answer one to three brief retrieval questions.
```

### Teacher Check (every lesson)

```
TEACHER CHECK
Complete: □
Date: __________
Initials: ________
```

## Lesson-Specific Rules

### Lessons 1–2

Custom instructional lessons aligned to Shurley objectives. Source: `source-materials/chapter-01/planning/lesson-01-02-planning.md`.

- Lesson 1: Discussion expectations, linking comments, goal setting. Ball-passing activity belongs in teacher edition.
- Lesson 2: Journaling, model letter, planning organizer, supported and less-scaffolded writing, envelope directions. Storage procedure belongs in teacher edition.

### Lessons 3–6

Preserve and improve confirmed content from `Chapter 1 Guided Notes.docx`.

- Lesson 3: Five parts of a complete sentence, synonyms, antonyms, blank vocabulary template.
- Lesson 4: Analogies (synonym, antonym, rhyming, part-to-whole), source analogy examples, blank vocabulary template.
- Lesson 5: Blank vocabulary template, capitalization practice, editing (no rule numbers).
- Lesson 6: Blank vocabulary template, capitalization and punctuation application, editing (no rule numbers).

## Capitalization and Punctuation

- Use error-count editing cues from Guided Notes where present.
- Do not invent capitalization rule lists or Shurley rule numbers.
- The Capitalization Reference Sheet referenced in Lesson 5 is not in the repository; direct teachers to supply it separately.

## Chapter Review

The In-Class Chapter Review appears at the end of the student packet **and** as separate files:

- `chapter-01-in-class-chapter-review.docx` (student-facing)
- `chapter-01-in-class-chapter-review-key.docx` (teacher key)

Requirements:

- Cover all major Chapter 1 skills.
- Not described as test preparation or a take-home study resource.
- Include one short application task.
- Review and review key must be separate DOCX files.

## Teacher Edition Rules

- Mirror student packet page order exactly (same page count).
- Preserve the same question numbering.
- Fill every blank with model or acceptable alternate answers, or note "teacher-assigned — answers will vary."
- Include concise teacher notes, oral prompts, timing, and common misconceptions.
- Ball-passing discussion activity (Lesson 1) and Letter to Future Me storage directions (Lesson 2) belong in the teacher edition.
