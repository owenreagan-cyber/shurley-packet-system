**Packet pages:** 10–12  
**Estimated time:** 40–50 minutes total

---

## Lesson Objective

Students will be able to:

1. Name and apply the **five parts of a complete sentence** (Capital letter, End mark, Complete sense, Subject, Verb).
2. Define **synonyms** (words with almost the same meaning) and **antonyms** (words with opposite meanings).
3. Identify synonym and antonym relationships in word pairs.
4. Complete a vocabulary template with word, definition, synonym, antonym, sentence, and drawing.

## Guided Practice

### Page 10 (~15 min)

- **Learn:** Ask students to recall the five parts of a complete sentence (hint: Sentence Jingle).
- **Chant:** Lead the Sentence Jingle together.
- **Remember box:** Review — Capital letter, End mark, Complete sense, Subject, Verb.
- **Practice Together:** Check each part on the checklist as a class when reading a sample sentence aloud.
- **Learn — Synonyms/Antonyms:** Read definitions and examples; have students give one additional example of each.

### Page 11 (~15 min)

- **Practice Together:** Work through synonym/antonym pairs 1–4 as a class; students complete 5–8 with a partner.
- **Answers:** 1. Antonym  2. Synonym  3. Synonym  4. Antonym  5. Synonym  6. Synonym  7. Antonym  8. Synonym
- **Check understanding:** Ask students to explain *why* each pair is a synonym or antonym.

---

## Independent Practice

### Page 11 (continued)

- Students write **one synonym pair** and **one antonym pair** of their own.
- Accept any valid pairs; coach students who reverse the definitions.

### Page 12 (~15–20 min)

- Assign a vocabulary word (teacher choice).
- Students complete the blank template: word, definition, synonym, antonym, sentence, drawing.
- **Memory Check:** Students answer independently, then check with a partner.

---

## Common Student Mistakes

| Mistake | What to Do |
|---------|------------|
| Lists sentence parts but can't apply them when writing | Model writing one sentence and checking all five parts aloud |
| Confuses synonyms and antonyms | Use gesture cues: "same" (hands close) vs "opposite" (hands apart) |
| Circles wrong relationship on pairs | Have students say "These words mean almost the same" or "These words mean the opposite" before circling |
| Vocabulary sentence doesn't use the word correctly | Quick conference; ask student to read sentence aloud and self-correct |
| Forgets one vocabulary template field | Use template as a checklist before Teacher Check |

## Exit Check

Use the **Teacher Check** box on page 12:

1. Can the student name all **five parts** of a complete sentence?
2. Can the student define **synonym** and **antonym** in their own words?
3. Is the vocabulary template complete (word, definition, synonym, antonym, sentence, drawing)?

**Memory Check answers:**

- Five parts: Capital letter, End mark, Complete sense, Subject, Verb
- Synonym: words that have almost the same meanings
