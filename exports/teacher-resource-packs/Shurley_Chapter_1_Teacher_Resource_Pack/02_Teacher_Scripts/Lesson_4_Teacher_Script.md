# Chapter 1 — Lesson 4 Teacher Script

**Packet pages:** 5–7  
**Timing:** 35–45 minutes  
**Presentation:** `Ch.1_Lesson_4_Analogies.pptx`

---

## Understanding Analogies (Page 5)

**Teacher says:** "An analogy is a word puzzle. It shows how two pairs of words are related. Listen: front is to back as top is to bottom."

**Students do:** Repeat using "is to" and "as." Write **bottom** in the worked example.

**Expected responses:**
- Quick check (rhyme): **man**
- Worked example: **bottom** (Antonym relationship)

**Check for understanding:** Point to each Remember box item. Ask: "What does :: mean?" (is to ___ as)

**Misconception:** Students confuse : and ::.  
**Correction:** Single colon = one pair. Double colon = connects first pair to second pair.

---

## Four Relationships (Page 6)

**Teacher says:** "There are four common analogy relationships. Read one card aloud using 'is to' and 'as.'"

**Expected responses:** Synonym, Antonym, Rhyming, Part-to-Whole.

**Teacher model:** "big is to large as sound is to noise — that's a synonym analogy."

---

## Analogy Practice (Page 7)

**Teacher says:** "Work the first two analogies with a partner. Label the relationship before you fill in the blank."

**Expected responses:**
1. sad (Synonym)
2. awake (Antonym)
3. look/cook/hook (Rhyming)
4. book (Part-to-Whole)
- MC 1: **cold**  MC 2: **tall : short**

**Check for understanding:** "What relationship is tree : forest?" (Part-to-Whole)

**Misconception:** Students pick a word that fits but label the wrong relationship.  
**Correction:** Always name the relationship first, then choose the word.

**Also accept:** cook or hook for #3 if labeled Rhyming.

---

## Exit Check

- [ ] Can explain what :: means?
- [ ] Can name all four relationship types?
- [ ] Can complete one analogy with correct relationship label?
