## Punctuation (Page 13)

**Teacher says:** "Sentences need correct end marks, commas, and apostrophes. Read the model sentences aloud."

**Students do:** Read sentences 1 and 2. Identify capitals, commas, and end marks.

**Expected observations:**
- Sentence 1: question mark, commas in list, proper nouns capitalized
- Sentence 2: comma after direct address, proper noun capitalized

**Check for understanding:** "What end mark does a question need?" (Question mark)

---

## Editing Practice (Page 14)

**Teacher says:** "Correct capitals and punctuation. Count your corrections before you rewrite."

**Sentence 1:** yes mr green will move to denver colorado on june 18 2024  
**Expected:** Yes, Mr. Green will move to Denver, Colorado, on June 18, 2024.  
**Error count:** 6 capitals, 4 commas, 1 period, 1 end mark

**Sentence 2:** did your teacher ms coleman read charlottes web to your class today  
**Expected:** Did your teacher, Ms. Coleman, read Charlotte's Web to your class today?  
**Error count:** 5 capitals, 2 commas, 1 period, 1 end mark, 1 apostrophe

**Misconception:** Students forget apostrophe in possessives (Charlotte's).  
**Correction:** Point to reference Rule for apostrophes.

**Also accept:** Exclamation mark on Sentence 1 if student treats "Yes" as strong emotion.

**Check for understanding:** "Name three punctuation marks you used." (Period, comma, question mark, apostrophe, etc.)
