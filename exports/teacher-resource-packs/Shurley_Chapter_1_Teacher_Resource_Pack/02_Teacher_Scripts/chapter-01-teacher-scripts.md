## Page 2 — Lesson 1: Discussion and Goal Setting

### Answers
- Goal sort answers (see teacher edition for full list)

### Alternate Acceptable Answers
- Accept reasonable goal classifications with brief justification.

### Discussion Prompts
- Why do discussion rules help everyone learn?
- Turn and tell a partner one long-term goal.

**Timing:** 15–20 minutes for Learn and Practice Together

### Common Misconceptions
- Students may think any comment is 'on topic' even when it does not connect to the discussion.

### Procedures
- Ball-Passing Discussion Activity: Form a circle with students. Use a soft ball or stuffed object. The student who holds the ball shares one linking comment using a sentence starter from the page. Before tossing the ball, the student must link to what the previous speaker said. The next student repeats the pattern. Coach students who share without linking to use a starter. Run 2–3 rounds, then transition to written goal work.
