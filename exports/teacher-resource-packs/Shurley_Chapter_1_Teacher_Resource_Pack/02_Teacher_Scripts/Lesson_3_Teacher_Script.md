# Chapter 1 — Lesson 3 Teacher Script

**Packet pages:** 2–4  
**Timing:** 40–50 minutes total  
**Presentation:** `Ch.1_Lesson_3_Complete_Sentences.pptx`

---

## Opening (Page 2)

**Teacher says:** "Today we begin Chapter 1 with complete sentences. Think about the Sentence Jingle. What five things does every sentence need?"

**Expected responses:** Capital letter, end mark, complete sense, subject, verb (order may vary).

**Check for understanding:** Chant the Sentence Jingle together. Ask one student to name all five parts without looking.

**Misconception:** Students list parts but cannot apply them when writing.  
**Correction:** Model one sentence aloud and check each part: "I read a book." — capital I, period, makes sense, subject I, verb read.

---

## Practice Together (Page 2)

**Teacher says:** "Check each part on your page as we write a sentence together."

**Students do:** Check off Capital letter, End mark, Complete sense, Subject, Verb on the checklist.

**Apply:** Students write one complete sentence about their day and self-check all five parts.

---

## Synonyms and Antonyms (Page 3)

**Teacher says:** "Synonyms are words with almost the same meaning. Noisy and loud are synonyms. Antonyms are opposites — night and day."

**Expected responses:**
- Synonym pair example: run/dash, big/large
- Antonym pair example: hot/cold, up/down

**Guided practice answers:**
1. Antonym  2. Synonym  3. Synonym  4. Antonym  5. Synonym  6. Synonym  7. Antonym  8. Synonym

**Check for understanding:** "Are hot and warm synonyms or antonyms?" (Synonym)

**Misconception:** Students reverse synonym and antonym definitions.  
**Correction:** Gesture — hands close together (synonym/same), hands apart (antonym/opposite).

---

## Vocabulary Builder (Page 4)

**Teacher says:** "Choose one vocabulary word. Complete every section — definition, synonym, antonym, sentence, and memory picture."

**Expected responses:** Teacher-assigned word; answers vary.

**Check for understanding:** Student reads sentence aloud — does it use the word correctly?

**Correction points:**
- Sentence must use the vocabulary word in context.
- Drawing should connect to word meaning, not random art.

---

## Exit Check

- [ ] Can name all five sentence parts?
- [ ] Can define synonym and antonym?
- [ ] Vocabulary template complete?
