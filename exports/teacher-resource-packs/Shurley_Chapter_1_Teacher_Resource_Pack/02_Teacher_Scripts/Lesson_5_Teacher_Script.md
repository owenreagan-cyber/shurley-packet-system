# Chapter 1 — Lesson 5 Teacher Script

**Packet pages:** 11–12  
**Timing:** 30–40 minutes  
**Presentation:** `Ch.1_Lesson_5_Capitalization.pptx`

---

## Capitalization (Page 11)

**Teacher says:** "Proper nouns name specific people, places, or things. They always begin with a capital letter. Common nouns like 'friends' or 'night' do not."

**Students do:** Open reference pages 8–10. Write capital letters above words in Passage 1 and Passage 2.

**Expected responses — Passage 1:** My, I, Amy, Ford's, Theater, Friday, Nim's, Island  
**Expected responses — Passage 2:** My, Reagan, Beth, Main, Street, July

**Check for understanding:** "Should 'friends' be capitalized?" (No — common noun)

**Misconception:** Students capitalize every noun.  
**Correction:** Ask: "Is this a specific name or a general word?"

---

## Capitalization Editing (Page 12)

**Teacher says:** "Mark the capitals above each mistake. Then rewrite the full sentence correctly."

**Expected responses:**
1. We drove to Lake Michigan on Saturday with Mrs. Daniels.
2. Yes, Sam and I work on Saturdays at Quickfill Gas on Jasper Road in Denver.

**Correction points:**
- Lake Michigan = proper noun (place)
- Mrs. Daniels = proper noun (person/title)
- Quickfill Gas, Jasper Road, Denver = proper nouns

**Check for understanding:** "What letter does every sentence begin with?" (Capital)

---

## Exit Check

- [ ] Can define proper noun?
- [ ] Both editing sentences rewritten with correct capitals?
- [ ] Did not capitalize common nouns incorrectly?
