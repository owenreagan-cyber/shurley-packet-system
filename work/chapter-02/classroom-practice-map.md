## Lesson 9 — Classroom Practice 12: Sentence Blueprint + Revision

**Screenshot:** `…11.35.09 AM.png`, `…11.35.09+` (TM p88–91)

### Activity type

| Ex | Task |
|----|------|
| 1 | Original + Revised sentence + Revision Strategy code per word |
| 2 | Rewrite revised sentence + checklist |

### Revision strategies (Ref 32)

| Code | Name | Definition |
|------|------|------------|
| syn | Synonym | Replace with nearly same meaning |
| ant | Antonym | Replace with opposite |
| wc | Word Change | Replace with unrelated word |
| add | Added Word | Insert new word |
| delete | Deleted Word | Remove word |
| nc | No Change | Keep word |

### TM worked example

| | Labels | Sentence |
|---|--------|----------|
| Original | A Adj SN V Adv Adv Adv | The yellow leaves fell quickly down yesterday. |
| Revised | (add) Adj SN V Adv Adv Adv | Crisp golden leaves drifted slowly downward today. |

### Mover & Shaker (Ref 33)

- Revised: *Crisp golden leaves drifted slowly downward today.*
- Mover: *Today, crisp golden leaves drifted gently downward.*
- **Not for classification**
