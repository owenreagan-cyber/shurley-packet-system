## Universal Scripts

### Four Steps to Classify (Ref 13)

1. Ask the question  
2. Name the word  
3. Identify its job  
4. Label it  

**Sequence rule:** Subject Noun first → Verb → (Adverbs at verb) → (Adjectives at subject noun) → Article → declare Pattern → Skill Check (L7+)

### Verb question (always)

> What is being said about [subject]?

### Pattern declaration (L1–9)

> Subject Noun, Verb, Pattern 1.  
> Write **SN V / P1** in the pattern blank.

### Skill Check scripts (Ref 26 — L7+)

| Type | Script | Label |
|------|--------|-------|
| Declarative | Period, statement, declarative sentence. | **D** |
| Imperative | Period, command, imperative sentence. | **Imp** |
| Interrogative | Question mark, question, interrogative sentence. | **Int** |
| Exclamatory | Exclamation point, strong feeling, exclamatory sentence. | **E** |

**After Skill Check (Ref 27):**  
> Go back to the verb. Divide the complete subject from the complete predicate.  
> *(Draw slash directly before verb.)*

### Noun Check (Ref 29 — L8+)

> Subject Noun [word], yes, it is a noun.  
> Circle each noun in the classified sentence.

## Lesson 1 — Nouns, Verbs, Pattern 1

**TM pages:** 38–44, 43a | **Screenshots:** 01–05

### Objectives (TM)

Study Jingles 3–4; recognize nouns/verbs; Q&A Flow; Pattern 1; classify introductory sentences; CP4.

### Terminology

| Term | Definition (TM) |
|------|-----------------|
| Noun | Names person, place, thing, or idea |
| SN | Subject — who or what sentence is about |
| V | Shows action or being |
| Action verb | Shows action |
| Linking verb | Shows being (am, is, are, was, were…) |
| P1 | Pattern 1 = SN V |

### Sample S. — Ships sailed.

| Step | Question | Answer | Label |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | What sailed? | ships — Subject Noun | SN |
| 2 | What is being said about ships? | ships sailed — Verb | V |
| 3 | — | Subject Noun, Verb, Pattern 1 | SN V / P1 |

### Guided practice answers

**1. Plane flew.** — What flew? plane-SN / What is being said about plane? plane flew-V / P1

**2. Ladybugs crawled.** — What crawled? ladybugs-SN / … crawled-V / P1

**3. Boys raced.** — **Who** raced? boys-SN *(person → Who)* / … raced-V / P1

## Lesson 2 — Adverbs

**TM pages:** 45–49, 48a, 49a | **Screenshots:** 06–09

### Objectives

Jingle 5; study adverbs; classify introductory sentences; CP5; Journal 4.

### Terminology

| Term | TM script |
|------|-----------|
| Adverb | Modifies verb, adjective, or adverb |
| Adv questions | How? When? Where? |
| Go–Ask–Get | Go to verb (first) → Ask adverb question → Get adverb |

### Sample S. — Ships sailed slowly away today.

| Step | Question | Answer | Label |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| 1–2 | (SN, V as L1) | ships-SN, sailed-V | |
| 3 | Sailed how? | slowly — Adverb | Adv |
| 4 | Sailed where? | away — Adverb | Adv |
| 5 | Sailed when? | today — Adverb | Adv |
| 6 | — | Subject Noun, Verb, Pattern 1 | SN V / P1 |

### Guided practice answers

**1. Planes flew.** — P1 only (no adverbs)

**2. Planes flew quickly away today.** — Adv: quickly, away, today

**3. Spiders crawled around very quickly.** — Crawled **where?** around / **how?** quickly / **How quickly?** very *(Adv modifies Adv)*

## Lesson 3 — Adverb Practice + Sentence Blueprint

**TM pages:** 50–56, 50a–50b | **Screenshots:** 10–14

### Objectives

Classify practice sentences; five parts of complete sentence; build SN V Adv blueprint; Mover & Shaker; CP6.

### Five parts of a complete sentence

1. subject  
2. verb  
3. complete sense  
4. capital letter  
5. end mark  

### Practice sentence answers

**1. Bees buzzed angrily around today.** — SN bees / V buzzed / Adv angrily, around, today / P1

**2. Rabbits hopped around everywhere.** — SN rabbits / V hopped / Adv around, everywhere / P1

**3. Anthony listened very carefully yesterday.** — Who? Anthony-SN / V listened / Listened how? carefully / How carefully? **very** / when? yesterday / P1

### Mover & Shaker (Ref 18)

> Move adverb to front + comma. **Not for classification.**

*Cats prowled hungrily around yesterday.* → *Yesterday, cats prowled hungrily around.*

## Lesson 4 — Adjectives

**TM pages:** 58–62, 61a | **Screenshots:** 15–18

### Objectives

Jingle 6; adjectives; Q&A with Adj; classify introductory sentences; CP7.

### Terminology

| Term | TM |
|------|-----|
| Adjective | Modifies/describes noun or pronoun |
| Adj questions | What kind? Which one? How many? |
| Go–Ask–Get | Go to **subject noun** → ask adjective questions |

### Sample S. — Those five cargo ships sailed slowly away today.

| Step | Question | Answer | Label |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| 1–5 | SN, V, Adv (slowly, away, today) | ships, sailed, Adv… | |
| 6 | What kind of ships? | cargo | Adj |
| 7 | How many ships? | five | Adj |
| 8 | Which ships? | those | Adj |
| 9 | — | SN V P1 | |

**Order note:** Adverbs **before** adjectives in Q&A (TM Ref 19 flow).

### Guided practice answers

**1. That owl flew silently.** — Adv silently / Which owl? that-Adj

**2. That large white owl flew silently overhead tonight.** — Adv silently, overhead, tonight / Adj white, large, that

**3. Several little red ladybugs crawled everywhere yesterday.** — Adj red, little, several / Adv everywhere, yesterday

## Lesson 5 — Article Adjectives

**TM pages:** 63–67, 66a | **Screenshots:** 18–21

### Objectives

Jingle 7; a, an, the; a/an rules; classify sentences; CP8.

### Terminology

| Term | TM |
|------|-----|
| Article adjectives | a, an, the |
| Also known as | noun markers (jingle) |
| Label | **A** |
| a/an rule | a + consonant **sound**; an + vowel **sound** |
| Find articles | Memorize and recognize on sight |

### Sample S. — The five cargo ships sailed slowly away.

| Step | Question | Answer | Label |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| 1–7 | SN through Adj (cargo, five) | | |
| 8 | — | **The** — Article Adjective | A |
| 9 | — | SN V P1 | |

### a/an guided drill (GN + TM oral)

| Sentence | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| ___ tall tree | **a** |
| ___ unhappy baby | **an** |
| ___ flying owl / ___ owl | **a** / **a** |
| Did you find ___ oily cloth… | **an** |
| Would you like ___ fresh peach? | **a** |
| We saw ___ large animal… | **a** |
| We saw ___ anteater… | **an** |
| We saw ___ movie… / ___ exciting movie… | **a** / **an** |

### Guided classification answers

**1. The girls giggled loudly.** — A The / SN girls / V giggled / Adv loudly / P1

**2. The four happy girls giggled loudly today.** — A The / Adj happy, four / SN girls / V giggled / Adv loudly, today / P1

**3. A huge whale swam very slowly away.** — A A / Adj huge / SN whale / V swam / Adv slowly, very, away / P1

## Lesson 6 — Full-Pattern Classification + Blueprint

**TM pages:** 68–72, 68a–68b | **Screenshots:** 22–25

### Objectives

Classify practice sentences; build A Adj Adj SN V Adv blueprint; Mover & Shaker (-ed→-ing); CP9.

### Practice answers

**1. The nice young doctor spoke very kindly today.**  
A The / Adj nice, young / SN doctor / V spoke / Adv very, kindly, today / P1

**2. A little yellow duck waddled slowly away.**  
A A / Adj little, yellow / SN duck / V waddled / Adv slowly, away / P1

**3. An enormous black bug flew around today.**  
A An / Adj enormous, black / SN bug / V flew / Adv around, today / P1

### Blueprint example (Ref 22)

*The two stray cats prowled hungrily around yesterday.*

### Mover & Shaker (Ref 24)

Change **-ed → -ing** when moving to front + comma:  
*Prowling hungrily around yesterday, the two stray cats hunted.*
